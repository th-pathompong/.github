# 🌙 Middnight

> We believe the best moments happen when people create together. Middnight builds joyful, accessible experiences that turn communities into co-creators.

## 🎨 Our Vision

**Middnight** exists to transform how people experience entertainment—from passive consumption to active participation. We build joyful, accessible tools that empower communities to create together, whether through collaborative music production, shared creative spaces, or playful social experiences. 

Our projects are designed for everyone: from complete beginners to seasoned creators, from small friend groups to global communities. Because we believe the most meaningful moments happen when people don't just watch or listen—but co-create, experiment, and express themselves freely together.

## 🚀 Active Projects

### 🎵 [Jam Band](https://github.com/middnight/jam-band)

**Real-time collaborative music creation platform**

Jam Band transforms music creation into a social experience. Whether you're jamming live with friends across the globe or arranging detailed productions together, Jam Band makes collaborative music accessible to everyone.

**Key Features:**
- **🎸 Perform Mode** — Live jamming with real-time sync
- **🎹 Arrange Mode** — Detailed collaborative production
- **🔊 Built-in Instruments** — Synthesizers, drums, and effects
- **👥 Room System** — Private, band-only, or public sessions

**Tech Stack:** React, TypeScript, Node.js, Socket.IO, Redis, Prisma, Tone.js, Web Audio API

**Status:** 🟢 Active Development

At Middnight, we choose technologies that prioritize:

- **🎯 Accessibility** — Low barrier to entry, intuitive UX
- **⚡ Performance** — Real-time collaboration without lag

## 🗺️ What's Next

### 📍 Location-Based Social App (Concept)

Exploring a playful social platform where people can leave anonymous creative messages tied to physical locations—inspired by the spontaneous creativity found in public spaces. The idea: when people aren't tied to their identity, they express themselves more freely and creatively.

**Core Concept:**
- Visit real-world locations and discover what others have written
- Leave your own creative, playful, or humorous messages
- Anonymous expression within healthy boundaries
- Turn everyday spaces into canvases for shared creativity

**Status:** 💡 Early Concept / Exploration
